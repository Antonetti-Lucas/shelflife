Before you sync

In your Steam profile's Privacy Settings, set Game details to Public (and the wishlist too, if you want it read). A private profile shows no games.

The key is checked with Steam when you press the button, and nothing is saved if Steam does not accept it. It is never shown on a page again and never leaves your computer except to Steam. Don't share it or commit .env.
