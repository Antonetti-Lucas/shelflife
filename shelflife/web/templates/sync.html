What a sync does

  1. {{ steps[0][1] }}. One call to Steam's API: your games, hours and when you last played each.
  2. {{ steps[1][1] }}. Official API, quick. Only games you've played, and later syncs only the ones you played since.
  3. {{ steps[2][1] }}. The slow part, about 3 seconds a game, so the very first sync takes a while. Later syncs only handle games you've added.
  4. {{ steps[3][1] }}. For games you own but have barely played, so recommendations can weigh how well they are reviewed.

A sync saves as it goes. If you stop it, or Steam turns it away, the next one carries on where it left off. It never changes your diary. Family Sharing games and your wishlist are not part of it.
