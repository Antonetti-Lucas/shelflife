Add a game from another platform

For games you played on a console, another PC store or anywhere else, including family games you played somewhere other than Steam. You type its hours, as for family games. Haven't played it yet? Leave the hours empty and it goes on your backlog, recommended like an owned game you haven't started; played it but don't know the hours? Rate it or give it a status, and it stays off the recommendations.

A game on Steam too gets its details, tags and reviews from Steam. A game that isn't on Steam (an Atari, PlayStation or Wii game, say) comes from IGDB: its cover, description and tags made from IGDB's genres and themes, which you can change on its page. It teaches your taste like any game; ShelfLife never suggests games that aren't on Steam.
